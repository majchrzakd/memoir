Memoir

A fast, private notebook for Windows, Mac and iPhone. Your notes are encrypted on your device and kept in your own Google Drive — readable by you, and nobody else.

What it does

  • Folders and notes, with a simple editor: headings, lists, checklists, tables, images and attached files.
  • Links between notes, and a list of the notes that link to the one you're reading.
  • Replies under a note, a "Later" list with due dates, full-text search and note history.
  • Separate notebooks (workspaces), for example a personal one and a work one, each in its own Google Drive — and a password for any folder you'd rather keep out of sight.
  • Works offline. Changes sync between your PC, Mac and iPhone through your Google Drive.
  • Bring your notes over from Evernote, and export everything to plain Markdown files at any time (on Windows and Mac).

Download

  • Windows and Mac: get the installer (.exe) or the Mac app (.dmg) from the latest release. Memoir updates itself after that. On a Mac, open it the first time with right-click → Open.
  • iPhone: in testing on TestFlight; not in the App Store yet.

Private by design

Everything is encrypted on your device with a key protected by your passphrase, before it leaves your device. Google Drive only ever stores encrypted files. Memoir has no servers and no accounts; there is nothing for us to see.

Memoir asks Google for the narrowest access it offers (drive.file): it can see only the files it creates itself, in a Memoir folder — nothing else in your Drive.

Your notes stay yours

The file format is documented, and a small recovery tool turns a copy of your Memoir folder back into Markdown files with your passphrase or recovery key — even without the app.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Memoir is a notebook app for your computer. This policy explains what data it handles and what happens to it. In short: your notes are encrypted on your device, stored only on your device and in your own Google Drive, and never sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Memoir is made by Dominik Majchrzak. For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue at github.com/majchrzakd/memoir/issues.

What Memoir stores, and where

  • Your notes, folders and attachments are stored on your computer in an encrypted database, and — if you connect Google Drive — as encrypted files in a folder named Memoir in your own Google Drive.
  • They are encrypted on your computer with a key that only your passphrase (or recovery key) unlocks, before they are written to disk or uploaded. Google, the developer of Memoir and anyone else without your passphrase cannot read them.
  • On your computer, Windows Credential Manager holds the key to the local database (if you chose "Remember on this device") and the sign-in token for Google Drive.
  • A log file on your computer records what the app did (for example, sync rounds and errors) to help with troubleshooting. It never contains the content of your notes, and it is not sent anywhere.

Google user data

When you connect Google Drive, Memoir asks Google for one permission: see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file).

  • Memoir uses it to create the Memoir folder and to upload, download and delete its own encrypted files there. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Through the same permission it reads your Google account's email address, shown in Memoir's settings so you know which account is connected.

This data is used only to provide Memoir's syncing to you. It is transferred directly between your computer and Google; Memoir has no servers. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

Memoir's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Memoir does not do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network service Memoir talks to is Google (for sign-in and Drive), and only after you connect it.

Your control

  • Disconnect: Settings → Disconnect revokes Memoir's access to your Google account. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete: delete the Memoir folder in your Google Drive and uninstall the app; uninstalling leaves your local data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.memoir.notebook, which you can delete as well.
  • Export: Settings → Export to Markdown saves all your notes as plain files.

Children

Memoir is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
